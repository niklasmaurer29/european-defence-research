# FY2025 investment-case screen - evidence before recommendation

## Purpose

This is the first qualitative interpretation of the FY2025 fact base,
definition-aware credit evidence and 31 December 2025 valuation screen. It is
an educational research exercise, not investment advice and not a buy, hold or
sell recommendation.

## One-line positioning

| Company | Evidence-based positioning | What has to be true |
|---|---|---|
| Rheinmetall | Highest order visibility and strongest balance-sheet liquidity, priced at a 42.4x EV / EBIT screen. | The backlog must convert into profitable delivery without the premium valuation compressing. |
| Hensoldt | Structural defence-electronics exposure with 3.6x backlog / revenue, priced at 41.7x EV / EBIT. | Backlog conversion and earnings growth must offset its lower interest-coverage diagnostic. |
| RENK | Lowest EV / EBIT screen at 33.7x, with 4.9x backlog / revenue and 5.0x gross-interest coverage. | Working capital must normalise and free-cash-flow conversion must recover. |

## Company evidence cards

### Rheinmetall

**Core evidence**

- Revenue was EUR 9,935m and operating result was EUR 1,841m in FY2025.
- Stated backlog was EUR 63,800m, or 6.4x revenue.
- The 31 December 2025 valuation screen shows EUR 369m net liquidity and a
  42.4x EV / EBIT multiple.

**Catalyst to monitor:** execution against the backlog and continued conversion
into operating profit and cash flow.

**Principal risk:** the stated backlog includes framework agreements as well as
binding order backlog. The 6.4x ratio therefore overstates comparability with
the two peers if treated mechanically. The premium valuation also raises the
cost of disappointing delivery or margin execution.

### Hensoldt

**Core evidence**

- FY2025 revenue was EUR 2,455m, adjusted EBITDA EUR 452m and backlog
  EUR 8,833m, or 3.6x revenue.
- The 31 December 2025 EV / EBIT screen is 41.7x.
- Lease-inclusive standardised net debt is EUR 748m; EBIT / gross interest
  expense is 2.1x. Management separately reported 1.6x net leverage under its
  own covenant definition.

**Catalyst to monitor:** conversion of the order book into earnings while the
refinanced funding structure is maintained within its covenant terms.

**Principal risk:** the issuer-reported net-debt table excludes lease
liabilities, so a superficial reading understates the debt burden used in this
project's standardised analysis. Lower interest coverage gives less room for
operational underperformance.

### RENK

**Core evidence**

- FY2025 revenue was EUR 1,366m, adjusted EBIT EUR 230m and backlog
  EUR 6,680m, or 4.9x revenue.
- The 31 December 2025 EV / EBIT screen is 33.7x, the lowest of the three.
- Standardised net debt is EUR 348.2m and EBIT / gross interest expense is
  5.0x. Management reported a separate 1.7x covenant net-leverage ratio.
- Free cash flow was EUR 66.9m, lower than EUR 87.4m in FY2024, as working
  capital increased.

**Catalyst to monitor:** a recovery in free-cash-flow conversion as inventory,
trade receivables and contract assets normalise while the backlog converts.

**Principal risk:** a lower EV / EBIT screen alone is not a valuation thesis.
Persistent working-capital absorption would weaken cash conversion and could
justify a discount to peers.

## Diligence priority - not a recommendation

The evidence supports three different follow-up questions rather than a winner
label:

1. **Rheinmetall:** Is a 42.4x EV / EBIT screen supported by profitable backlog
   conversion after removing the framework-agreement comparability effect?
2. **Hensoldt:** Can earnings growth maintain comfortable debt service once
   lease liabilities and gross interest expense are considered?
3. **RENK:** Is the 33.7x EV / EBIT screen a genuine discount, or compensation
   for weaker FY2025 free-cash-flow conversion?

## How to explain this in an interview

> I did not jump from a multiple table to a stock call. I linked the valuation
> screen to operating evidence: backlog quality and conversion for Rheinmetall,
> debt service for Hensoldt, and working-capital-driven cash conversion for
> RENK. That created a focused diligence agenda rather than an unsupported
> recommendation.
