# Credit evidence - first audited extract

This note records what the annual-report extraction has established. It is not
a peer ranking: each company now has a first-pass audited credit extract, but
the issuer definitions are not interchangeable.

## Hensoldt - FY2025

| Item | Value | Annual-report page | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| EBIT | EUR 221m | 145 | Earnings before financial result and income taxes. |
| Interest expense | EUR 106m | 145 | Gross interest expense reported in the consolidated income statement. |
| Interest income | EUR 25m | 145 | Separate reported income item. |
| Net interest expense before other finance items | EUR 81m | Calculated from p. 145 | EUR 106m less EUR 25m; other finance expense of EUR 13m remains excluded from this narrow interest measure. |
| Reported net debt | EUR -297m | 212 | Net cash under Hensoldt's own reported definition. |
| Lease liabilities | EUR 451m | 148 | EUR 416m non-current plus EUR 35m current; excluded from Hensoldt's reported net-debt table. |

### Transparent, standalone calculations

- **EBIT / gross interest expense:** 221 / 106 = **2.1x**.
- **EBIT / net interest expense before other finance items:** 221 / (106 - 25)
  = **2.7x**.

These are Hensoldt-only diagnostic calculations, not a peer comparison. The
annual report also discloses **1.6x net leverage**, but that is a covenant
measure based on "consolidated EBITDA" as defined in the syndicated-loan
agreement. It should not be reconciled mechanically to the IFRS line items
above.

## Rheinmetall - FY2025

| Item | Value | Annual-report page | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| EBIT | EUR 1,684m | 199 | Earnings before interest and taxes. |
| Interest expense | EUR 116m | 199 | Gross interest expense in the consolidated income statement. |
| Interest income | EUR 4m | 199 | Separate reported income item. |
| Net interest expense | EUR 112m | Calculated from p. 199 | EUR 116m less EUR 4m; the EUR 7m other financial result remains excluded. |
| Reported net liquidity | EUR 369m | 60 | Cash and cash equivalents of EUR 1,650m less financial debts of EUR 1,281m. |
| Lease liabilities | EUR 306m | 226 / 240 | Included within Rheinmetall's total financial debts. |

### Transparent, standalone calculation

- **EBIT / net interest expense:** 1,684 / 112 = **15.0x**.

This is a Rheinmetall-only diagnostic calculation, not a peer comparison.
Rheinmetall's total financial debts include leases, whereas Hensoldt's reported
net-debt table excludes them. The next stage will standardise the lease
treatment across all issuers before comparing their debt burden.

## RENK - FY2025

| Item | Value | Annual-report page | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| EBIT | EUR 169.4m | 140 | Reported as operating profit. |
| Interest expense | EUR 33.8m | 140 / 176 | Gross interest expense; no separate interest-income line is reported. |
| Total financial liabilities | EUR 535.3m | 196 | Includes loans, cash-pool liabilities and lease liabilities. |
| Cash and cash equivalents | EUR 187.1m | 142 | Consolidated statement of financial position. |
| Lease liabilities | EUR 18.5m | 196 | Included within total financial liabilities. |
| Standardised net debt | EUR 348.2m | Calculated from pp. 142 and 196 | EUR 535.3m less EUR 187.1m; transparent project calculation, not an issuer-reported metric. |
| Covenant net leverage | 1.7x | 196 | Issuer-specific consolidated covenant ratio at 31 December 2025. |

### Transparent, standalone calculation

- **EBIT / gross interest expense:** 169.4 / 33.8 = **5.0x**.

RENK cannot yet be shown using EBIT / net interest expense because the annual
report does not present a separate interest-income line. For the preliminary
cross-company view, retain this as a gross-interest-expense diagnostic.

## Definition-aware credit view

| Company | Balance-sheet net debt view | Interest-coverage diagnostic | Key caution |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Rheinmetall | EUR -369m standardised net debt (net liquidity) | 14.5x EBIT / gross interest expense | Interest expense includes financing components; financial debts include leases. |
| Hensoldt | EUR 748m standardised net debt | 2.1x EBIT / gross interest expense | Issuer-reported net-debt table excludes leases; 1.6x leverage is a separate covenant metric. |
| RENK | EUR 348.2m standardised net debt | 5.0x EBIT / gross interest expense | No separately disclosed interest income; 1.7x leverage is a separate covenant metric. |

This is a definition-aware screening view, not a credit score or investment
recommendation. It is appropriate to discuss the evidence and caveats in an
interview, but not to claim that one issuer has the "best" credit profile.
